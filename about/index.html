The bench

I make records.
These are the tools
I needed.

Ferric is one person in San Diego — a producer who spends his days in this room, and builds instruments in the hours around it. Everything on this site comes off one bench.

01Why any of this

Made by somebody who uses them

I have been making music my whole life — guitars, drums, synths, a sampler, an oud, and a room that has slowly filled up with gear that earned its wear. Producing is what I do with my days. These instruments come out of that, not out of a product meeting.

Somewhere in the middle of making a record I noticed the obvious thing: the most powerful machine I owned was the one in my pocket, and there was nothing on it I actually wanted to play. Plenty of apps — but apps that demo well and teach nothing, that keep nothing worth keeping, that treat a phone like a toy because it happens to be small.

Meanwhile the hardware in this room was doing the opposite. Teaching me what a filter does by letting me turn it. Keeping the 2 a.m. take. Getting better as it wore in. I wanted that on the thing I always have with me.

So I started building it. One instrument at a time, one panel at a time, in the hours around the records.

“If somebody leaves one of these understanding spring reverb well enough to go buy a real tank, the app worked.”

02What the shop is for

Closer to the instrument, not further from it

The goal is not to replace the gear in this room. It is to bring people closer to it — to the instruments, and to the part of making something where you are actually deciding. If one of these sends somebody out to buy a real amp, a real synth, a real four-track, that is the best outcome I can think of.

INSPIRE, BOTH WAYS

I build these to get myself unstuck as much as anyone else. Every one exists because a record needed it.

TEACH BY PLAYING

Labelled panels, no menus, no tutorials. You learn what a control does the way you always have — by turning it and listening.

REACHABLE, AND LASTING

Priced so a kid with a used phone can own the shelf, free to anyone who asks, and bought once. No subscription, no account, works offline forever.

03The proof

These are practice tools for a real project

I release music as SALTON — desert psych, atmospheric, made on exactly this gear. The records are the demo. If the tools were not good enough to finish real songs on, I would find out long before you did.
