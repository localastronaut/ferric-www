The bench

One person,
a room,
and the gear.

Ferric is a small instrument works in San Diego. Small meaning one. Everything on this site — the DSP, the panels, the field manuals, the drawings, this page — comes off one bench.

PORTRAITpublic/img/about/portrait.jpg
01Why any of this

It started as an annoyance

I am a musician first. Guitar, synths, a sampler, an oud, a room full of gear that has been slowly earning its wear. Somewhere in the middle of making a record I noticed the obvious thing: the most powerful machine in the room was the one in my pocket, and it had nothing real on it.

Not nothing — plenty of apps. But apps that demo well and teach nothing, that keep nothing you would want back, that treat a phone like a toy because it is small. Meanwhile the actual hardware I loved was doing the opposite: teaching me what a filter does, keeping the 2 a.m. take, getting better as it wore in.

So I started building the instruments I wanted. Not emulations of the gear on my desk — the thing that gear would have become if it had been born on glass. One at a time, one panel at a time, in the hours after the day job.

“If somebody leaves one of these understanding spring reverb well enough to go buy a real tank, the app worked.”

02What the shop is for

Three things I'm trying to get right

MAKE IT REAL

Instruments, not idea machines. Nothing here writes the song for you — you play them, and playing is how they teach.

MAKE IT REACHABLE

Priced so a kid with a used phone can own the whole shelf, and free to anyone who writes and says they can't afford it.

MAKE IT LAST

Bought once. No subscription, no account, no telemetry, works offline forever. If the shop goes quiet, your instruments don't.

03The proof

These are practice tools for a real project

I make music as SALTON — desert psych, atmospheric, made on exactly this gear. Every instrument in the line exists because a record needed it. The records are the demo, and if the tools were not good enough to finish real songs on, I would know before you did.
