01Why it exists

The front door is free.

Every player already needs a tuner. It is the one music app with no audience problem — so the first Ferric instrument costs nothing. No ads, no unlocks, no nag screen. The restraint is the advertisement.

Getting in tune takes twenty seconds, and most tuners stop there. S–7 is about what happens next: pick a key and the whole neck lights up with the notes that belong to it — see the scale, hear its chords, play it until it is yours.

It teaches quietly. No course, no streak, no cartoon owl — a labelled panel, the way hardware has always taught. It is for the kid with a used phone and a pawn-shop guitar as much as for the player wiring a pickup straight into the plug.

03The field manual

Tune. See. Hear. Learn.

Tune

A 15-LED needle, flat left, sharp right. Green at centre. It comes up listening — a tuner you have to switch on is a tuner you are already fighting. Phone mic, or the plug when you press INPUT; a pickup wired straight in reads truer than a room full of reflections.
FLAT — WALK IT RIGHT GREEN AT CENTRE. WITHIN ±4¢. SHARP — WALK IT LEFT

See

Choose a key and a scale — major, minor, both pentatonics, blues, and the modes — and the neck or the keys light up with what belongs. Capo up to 9 frets and the shapes move with you.

Hear

Every chord in the key, laid out with its numeral. Tap to hear it. Tap again and the neck narrows to a real fingering — dots and ✕, open position or barre.
IC iiDm iiiEm IVF VG viAm vii°B°

Learn

One page. No modes, no tabs, no settings screen, nothing to save. The needle is pinned at the top and never scrolls away; everything else rides under it.
NEEDLEPINNED ▲ ALWAYS VISIBLE · ▼ SCROLLS KEYWHAT YOU ARE IN CHORDSWHAT FITS THE MAPNECK OR KEYS — YOUR INSTRUMENT

The whole manual ships inside the app. Tap the wordmark — it is the only help there is, and the only help you need.
