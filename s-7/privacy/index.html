S–7 · Privacy policy

S–7 collects nothing.

  • MICROPHONES–7 listens so it can tune you. Audio is analyzed on the device, in real time, and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted. When the app closes, nothing remains.
  • DATA COLLECTEDNone. No analytics, no telemetry, no identifiers, no third-party code, no server. The App Store label reads “Data Not Collected” because there is nothing to collect it with.
  • ACCOUNTThere isn't one. Nothing to sign into, nothing to reset, nothing to leak.
  • NETWORKS–7 makes no network connections. It works offline, permanently.
  • CHANGESIf any of the above ever changes, this page changes first — and the change will be called out plainly, not buried.

Questions: this page is the policy, and the FIELD MANUAL inside the app is the support surface. Effective from the first App Store release of S–7.
