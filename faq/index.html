FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Last updated 5 August 2026
What is Ferric, in one sentence?+

A small instrument works making music apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac that behave like hardware — labelled panels, no menus, nothing to subscribe to.

Do I need to know music theory?+

No. The panels teach while you play, the way a labelled piece of hardware always has. S-7 will show you which notes belong to a key on your own fretboard, which is theory you can see and hear rather than read.

Is anything a subscription?+

No. Every instrument is a one-time purchase, and every add-on is a non-consumable that stays on your shelf. Nothing renews and nothing expires.

What does $9.99 actually cover?+

One App Store purchase covers that instrument on your iPhone and iPad. The Mac app and the AUv3 plugin come in THE FERRIC RACK, a separate $79 purchase that covers every instrument on the desk.

Do the apps work offline?+

Completely. There is no server check, no licence validation and no account. If the shop went quiet tomorrow, everything you own would keep working.

Do you collect my data or my recordings?+

No. There is no analytics, no telemetry and no third-party SDK in any instrument, and no Ferric server for anything to go to. Audio is processed on your device and never uploaded.

Can I use these in a DAW?+

Yes. Every instrument ships as an AUv3, so it loads in Logic, GarageBand, AUM and anything else that hosts Audio Units on macOS or iOS.

Can I plug in a real guitar or a MIDI keyboard?+

Yes. Any class-compliant audio interface or MIDI controller works. Wire a guitar through an interface into D-65, or play J-17 from a keyboard. On a phone you will usually want a powered hub, since the phone alone often cannot supply enough power.

What do I need to run them?+

A reasonably current iPhone or iPad, or a Mac. Exact minimum OS versions are listed on each App Store page at release. Headphones or an interface will always sound better than the built-in speaker.

Can I sell music I make with these?+

Yes, without asking and without paying us anything. Your recordings are yours. That is the entire point.

How do I get into the beta?+

Join the list. Beta invites go out through TestFlight, free, before anything ships.

What if I cannot afford it?+

Write to scholarships@ferric.works and we send you a code. No application, no proof of income, no essay. A price should never be the reason somebody does not find out whether they are a musician.

Anything else — support, or write to hello@ferric.works. A person answers.
